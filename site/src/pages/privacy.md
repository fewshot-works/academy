# Privacy Notice

**Effective and last updated: August 30, 2026**

Few-Shot Academy is responsible for the website data described here. For privacy questions or a
request concerning information you submitted, email
[contact@fewshotacademy.com](mailto:contact@fewshotacademy.com) or use the [contact form](/contact).

## At a glance

- You do not need an account, and the website does not collect payment-card information.
- Google Analytics is optional. Its tag does not load unless you select **Allow analytics**.
- Course progress and quiz results stay in your browser and are not synchronized to our server.
- Contact and feedback submissions are stored so they can be read and acted on.
- Blog comments connect to GitHub only after you select **Load GitHub comments**.

## What is collected

| Activity | Information | Why it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visit a curriculum or career page | Page path, aggregate view count, and last-viewed time | Identify useful and underused pages without creating a visitor profile |
| Allow optional analytics | Page visits, session and device/browser information, approximate location, and selected course interactions | Understand learner journeys and improve the curriculum |

## Optional Google Analytics

Few-Shot Academy uses Google Analytics 4 only after you allow it. Google may then process page
addresses, a cookie-based client identifier, session statistics, approximate location, and
browser/device information. Google Analytics can set `_ga` and related `_ga_*` cookies, which may
remain for up to two years unless you clear them or withdraw your choice.

The site also records selected interactions, such as starting or completing a lesson, following
the next-lesson link, submitting a quiz, opening a lab, or moving between blog, career, interview,
and curriculum pages. These custom events use stable content identifiers. They do not contain:

- Quiz answers or scores
- Contact or feedback messages
- Email addresses
- The contents of locally saved course progress
- A custom account or user identifier
- URL query strings or fragments

Advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalization are denied in the
site's Google tag configuration. Read [Google's description of Analytics data collection](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can decline analytics and still use the entire curriculum. Use **Privacy settings** in the
footer to change your choice. Withdrawing disables future analytics activity and removes Google
Analytics cookies that the website can access. Clearing browser data also removes your saved
choice, so the site will ask again.

## Aggregate page counts

For curriculum and career pages, the server increments a first-party aggregate counter. It stores
the page path, total count, and last-viewed time. It does not add an IP address, cookie, browser
identifier, or other visitor identifier to that counter.

## Contact and feedback submissions

Contact submissions are stored in Cloudflare D1. If you provide an email, it is used to respond
to that submission. Email is optional. A copy of the submission is sent through Cloudflare Email
Routing to the Few-Shot Academy contact inbox.

Feedback submissions are also stored in Cloudflare D1 and copied to the contact inbox. The
feedback form does not request an email address. Avoid placing contact details or sensitive
information in its free-text fields.

There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for contact or feedback database records or
their notification emails. They are retained unless you request deletion or a deletion process is
introduced. To request deletion, email
[contact@fewshotacademy.com](mailto:contact@fewshotacademy.com) with enough information to locate
the submission. This notice will be updated when an automatic retention process is established.

## Cloudflare Turnstile

The contact page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to reduce automated spam. The application sends the
challenge token and connecting IP address to Cloudflare for verification. Cloudflare may process
browser and environment signals for this security check. The application does not store the IP
address in its contact or feedback database tables.

Read the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and
[Turnstile documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/).

## Browser storage

The site uses browser storage to remember course progress, completed quiz results, theme choice,
and whether optional analytics is allowed. This information stays on that browser unless the
relevant feature explicitly says otherwise. It is not synchronized between devices. Clearing site
data removes it.

This functional storage remains available when analytics is declined because it provides features
you request, such as remembering completed lessons.

## GitHub comments

Blog comments are provided by Giscus and GitHub Discussions. The embed does not load
automatically. If you select **Load GitHub comments**, your browser connects to GitHub and GitHub
may process account, cookie, device, and usage information under the
[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Service providers and international access

Google processes optional analytics. Cloudflare hosts the site infrastructure, stores form
submissions, routes notification email, and provides Turnstile. GitHub hosts the source repository
and, when requested, blog comments. These providers may process information in countries other
than the one where you live under their own terms and transfer mechanisms.

Course labs may let you choose external model providers. Inputs you send from a lab go to the
provider you choose, not through the Few-Shot Academy website. See the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer)
and that provider's privacy terms before sending sensitive information.

## Your choices and requests

You can:

- Decline optional analytics without losing access to the site.
- Change or withdraw the analytics choice through **Privacy settings** in the footer.
- Clear local progress, quiz results, and preferences through your browser's site-data controls.
- Ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted by contacting
  [contact@fewshotacademy.com](mailto:contact@fewshotacademy.com).
- Contact your local data-protection authority if you believe applicable privacy rights were not
  respected.

We may need enough information to find the relevant submission and avoid deleting someone else's
information. We will not ask for more identification than is reasonably needed for that purpose.

## Children

Few-Shot Academy does not require accounts and is not designed to collect personal information
from children. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact us so it can be
reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

## Security and changes

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit and protect collected information, but no online
service can guarantee absolute security. This notice will be updated when data practices or
service providers materially change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
